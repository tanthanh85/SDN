# PetroVale Chapter 4 Campus Assurance and Troubleshooting Lab

After the successful ACI deployment, PetroVale management approved a Catalyst Center assurance-only pilot for the existing brownfield campus. Learners explore an instructor-provided Catalyst Center instance containing sample wired and wireless data. No SDA fabric or production provisioning is performed.

Run with `python3 -m http.server 8768 --bind 127.0.0.1`, then open `http://127.0.0.1:8768/`.
